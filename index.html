Privacy Policy for PixelForge

PixelForge is designed from the ground up to process your images entirely on your device, with zero cloud uploads and zero personal data collection.

App Name: PixelForge Package ID: com.aistudio.pixelforge.mkrtxz Effective Date: November 14, 2026

1. Overview & Zero-Data Commitment

PixelForge ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. All image-processing features—including the Image Compressor, Format Converter, Resizer, Crop & Rotate, Favicon Pack Generator, Color Palette Lab, Meme Studio, and EXIF & Privacy Cleaner—run 100% locally on your Android device.

We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, photos, generated assets, or usage analytics.

2. Photos, Media Access & Permissions

PixelForge follows Android and Google Play data-minimization best practices:

  • Zero Broad Storage Permissions: The App does not request READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, camera, microphone, or location permissions.
  • Android Photo Picker: When you choose an image, PixelForge uses the official system Android Photo Picker (PickVisualMedia). The App can only read the specific image(s) you explicitly select for that session.
  • Local Saving & Sharing: Exported images, icons, and favicon ZIP archives are written locally to your device via Android MediaStore (under Pictures/PixelForge or Downloads/PixelForge) or the Storage Access Framework when you choose a destination. When you tap Share, a temporary file is shared locally using Android's FileProvider with the app you select.

3. EXIF & Image Metadata

When you open a photo in PixelForge, the App reads its orientation tag locally so portrait photos display upright. In the EXIF & Privacy tool, embedded metadata (such as camera model, capture timestamp, and GPS coordinates, if present) is inspected solely on your device so you can review and remove it. No EXIF or GPS data ever leaves your phone.

4. Third-Party Services, Ads & Analytics

  • No Advertisements: PixelForge contains no ad networks or tracking pixels.
  • No Accounts or Logins: You do not need to create an account or sign in to use any feature.
  • No Analytics or Cloud Telemetry: PixelForge does not integrate third-party analytics SDKs, crash-reporting trackers, or remote AI/cloud APIs.

5. Clipboard Access

In tools such as Color Palette Lab and Favicon Pack, PixelForge writes color codes (HEX, CSS, JSON) or HTML <link> tags to your device clipboard only when you explicitly tap a Copy button. The App never reads your clipboard in the background.

6. Children's Privacy

Because PixelForge operates completely offline and collects no personal data from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if new features are added to PixelForge in future releases. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised Effective Date.
